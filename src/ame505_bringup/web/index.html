Just drive (no map)

Starts motors, lidar, camera and Xbox teleop. Then hold LB and use the sticks.

Make a 2D map

1
Start mapping

Put the robot at the spot you want as home (navigation will assume it starts here). Name the map, then press Start. The map grows live in the Map panel →

2
Drive around slowly
  • Hold LB to move (RB = faster). Left stick: drive and strafe. Right stick: turn.
  • Go slowly (no RB) and turn slowly. Come back to the start to close the loop.
  • Watch the Map panel: grey = unknown. Drive until every room / corridor you will patrol is white with black walls.
3
Stop & save

Saves the 2D map (maps_2d/<name>.pgm + .yaml). For a 3D map it also saves the RTAB-Map database and builds the 3D scene (~1 min).

4
View the 3D map

Patrol

First pick a map and press Start navigation in the Map panel →

  • Drive to each inspection spot (hold LB), aim the camera, and press Y on the controller or + Checkpoint here on the map (X = undo).
  • Then start the patrol. The robot visits every checkpoint and stops at each one.

GTSAM SLAM (lidar + gyro)

Runs next to driving, mapping or navigation (read-only, never drives). Keep the robot still ~5 s after starting. The orange path is GTSAM, drawn on your saved map.
